About

Data first. Then the decision.

32262.com turns open postal data into fast, free tools — and helps people make one of life's most expensive decisions, a move, with real numbers instead of sales pitches.

What we believe

  • Free and fast. Every tool runs in your browser with no sign-up.
  • Show the math. Every estimate lists its assumptions and sources.
  • Protect the mover. We only match requests with registered movers and cap contacts at five.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships, referral fees from moving and home-service partners when you request quotes, and reader support. None of these change our numbers. See our disclosure.

Independence

We are not affiliated with the United States Postal Service, any government agency, or any business that shares the digits 32262.
