What a lookup tells you

For US ZIPs we show the place name, state, coordinates, the region encoded in the first digit, the sectional center facility (SCF) encoded in the first three digits, the time zone with local time, a map, and the 12 nearest ZIP codes. For other countries we show every locality that shares the code.

Why some ZIPs return “not found”

Open datasets cover standard and most PO-box ZIPs. Unique ZIPs assigned to a single large organization, some military codes and brand-new or retired ZIPs may be missing. Unassigned numbers like 32262 never match.
