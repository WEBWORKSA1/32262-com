The 32262 story

We researched one five-digit number across postal systems, cultures, mathematics and the domain economy. Here is everything 32262 means — and why it became a home for ZIP intelligence.

32262

1 · In the US postal system: the ZIP that isn't

The first digit, 3, places 32262 in the national area covering Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Mississippi and Tennessee. The first three digits, 322, belong to the sectional center that serves Jacksonville, Florida — 56 active ZIPs share that prefix in open data.

But 32262 itself is not an active ZIP code. It does not appear among the 42,555 ZIPs in the open zipcodes dataset, nor in Jacksonville listings from major ZIP directories, which jump from 32260 (Jacksonville, PO box) to 32266 (Neptune Beach) and 32267. Gaps like this are normal: USPS holds numbers in reserve for growth, retires codes, and assigns some to single organizations.

That makes 32262 a fitting name for an independent ZIP intelligence site: a number that sits inside the system without belonging to any one neighborhood.

2 · In France: a village in the Gers

France's statistics office (INSEE) gives every commune a five-digit code. 32262 is Monbrun, a commune in the Gers département (département number 32) in Occitanie, whose postal code is 32600. Its neighbors in the code list are Monblanc (32261) and Moncassin (32263).

4 · In culture and numerology tradition, not fact

  • Western (Pythagorean) numerology reduces 3+2+2+6+2 = 15 → 1+5 = 6, traditionally “the number of home, family and responsibility” — a neat fit for a site about where people live and move.
  • Chinese number symbolism: 2 (二) is linked to pairs and the saying “good things come in pairs”; 6 (六, liù) sounds like 流 (“flowing, smooth”); 3 (三) is associated in Cantonese with 生 (“life, birth”). The string contains no 4 (四), whose sound resembles “death” — so readers in that tradition generally see it as pleasant.
  • The repeated 2 appears three times — symmetric, easy to say (“thirty-two, two-sixty-two”) and easy to remember, the qualities that matter most for a numeric brand.
  • As a date: 3/22/62 reads as Thursday, 22 March 1962 in US notation.

5 · In the economy

Five-digit postal codes quietly price much of daily life: in the US, ZIP codes feed insurance rating, sales-tax lookup, delivery zones, ad targeting, real-estate search and census statistics. Moving is one of the largest ZIP-driven purchases — industry guides put a 2–3 bedroom long-distance move at roughly $4,500–$8,500, and lead buyers (movers, agents, insurers) pay meaningful fees for verified, ZIP-specific customer requests.

In the domain market, all 100,000 five-digit numeric .com names are registered; demand is strongly driven by Chinese-speaking buyers who value numbers for sound and symbolism (and often avoid 4s).
