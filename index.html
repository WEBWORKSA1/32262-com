Frequently asked

Is 32262 a real ZIP code?

Not currently. 32262 falls in the 322 sectional-center block that serves Jacksonville, Florida, but it does not appear among the 42,555 ZIPs in open datasets — the active neighbors are 32260 (Jacksonville, PO box) and 32266 (Neptune Beach). More.

Where does your data come from?

US ZIP coordinates come from the open-source zipcodes dataset (BSD license); worldwide postal codes from Zippopotam.us (GeoNames, ODbL); maps from OpenStreetMap. See data & methodology.

Are you the Postal Service?

No. 32262.com is an independent site. For official addressing, use USPS tools. ZIP Code™ is a USPS trademark.

How do the free mover quotes work?

You submit one short form; we pass your request only to USDOT-registered movers serving your route (or to you directly by email if you ask). Our partners may pay us a referral fee — it never costs you anything.
