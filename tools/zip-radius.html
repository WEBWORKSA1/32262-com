ZIP codes within a radius

Every ZIP within X miles of a center ZIP — for delivery zones, ad targeting, direct mail and service areas.

First search downloads the 42,555-ZIP dataset (about 1 MB compressed) once; later searches are instant.

Use cases

  • Local businesses: define a delivery or service area and paste ZIPs into Google or Meta ad targeting.
  • Direct mail: build an EDDM-style ZIP list around a store.
  • Home search: list every ZIP within commuting range of a new job.

Distances are between ZIP center points; a ZIP whose area touches the circle but whose center lies outside it is not listed.
