Three rules landlords and planners use — side by side, with the lowest as your safe target.

The rules explained

30% rule: housing at or below 30% of gross monthly income — the long-standing US benchmark for “cost-burdened” households. 40× test: many landlords require annual income of at least 40 times monthly rent. DTI cap: keeping total debt payments, including rent, near 43% of gross income leaves room for savings.
