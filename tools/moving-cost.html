Moving cost estimator

Three ways to move, priced for your route and home size — built on published 2026 industry benchmarks.

Assumptions & sources

  • Local moves (<100 mi) are priced hourly at $100–$200/hour for a crew, 2–12 hours depending on home size, plus drive time (Opendoor; Storage Scholars 2026).
  • Long-distance moves are priced by weight × distance. We assume ~2,000 lb (studio) to ~10,000 lb (4+ BR), at $0.65–$1.00/lb around 1,000 miles, scaled by distance. Published examples: 2–3 BR over 1,000+ miles ≈ $4,500–$8,500.
  • Peak season (May–September) adds 15–30%.
  • DIY truck: ~$335 for a 10-mile local move to ~$3,250 for 1,500 miles, before your time.
  • Packing adds $200–$1,500; Full Value Protection costs roughly 1–2% of declared value. Basic coverage is only 60¢ per pound per item.

Estimates are planning ranges, not offers. Only a written estimate from a registered mover is a price.
