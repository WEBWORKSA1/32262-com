Straight-line distance, estimated road miles, drive time and typical moving cost between any two US ZIPs.

How we calculate

We use the haversine formula on each ZIP's center point (Earth radius 3,958.8 mi). Road distance is estimated at 1.2× straight-line — a common rule of thumb for US road networks — and drive time at an average 55 mph. For exact routing use a navigation app.

Moving-cost ranges come from our estimator, which benchmarks published 2026 industry pricing.
