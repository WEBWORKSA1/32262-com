Who uses 5-digit postal codes?

Besides the United States, five-digit codes are used in Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Mexico, Türkiye, Finland, Malaysia, Thailand, Croatia, Guatemala, Pakistan, Sri Lanka and the Dominican Republic, among others; Sweden, Czechia and Slovakia write theirs as “123 45”. The same number can therefore be a Spanish village and a US suburb at once.
