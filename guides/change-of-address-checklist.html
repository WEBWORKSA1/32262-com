Change-of-address checklist

Update in this order: the things that forward mail first, then the things that can fine you, then everything else.

2–4 weeks before

  • Mail forwarding with your postal service (in the US, USPS offers online change of address for a small identity-verification fee — beware look-alike sites charging more).
  • Employer / payroll
  • Banks, credit cards, loans and investment accounts
  • Insurance: auto, home/renters, health, life
  • Utilities: stop at the old address, start at the new one; internet installation can take 1–2 weeks

Within 30 days after

  • Tax agency (in the US, IRS Form 8822)
  • Driver's license and vehicle registration — many states require updates within 10–30 days
  • Voter registration
  • Social security, pensions, benefits; veterans' services
  • Doctors, dentist, pharmacy; transfer prescriptions
  • Schools and childcare

When you get to it

  • Online shopping accounts and saved addresses
  • Subscriptions, memberships, gyms, clubs
  • Professional licenses and associations
  • Friends and family
