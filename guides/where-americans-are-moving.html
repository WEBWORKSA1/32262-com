Where Americans are moving

United Van Lines' 49th annual National Movers Study tracks the share of each state's interstate moves that were inbound. In 2025 the leaders were:

#StateInbound share
1Oregon65%
2West Virginia62%
3South Carolina61%
4Delaware60%
5Minnesota58%
6Idaho58%
7North Carolina58%
8Arkansas57%
9Alabama57%
10Nevada57%

The study notes these destinations share relatively attainable housing and shorter commutes. For the first time in more than a decade, Texas and Florida recorded nearly equal inbound and outbound moves, and Illinois returned to balance.

Why people move

  • To be closer to family — 29% of interstate moves, the top reason for five years running.
  • New job or transfer — 26%.
  • Retirement — 14%.
