What moving costs in 2026

Moving prices follow two different models. Know which one applies and you can predict — and cut — your bill.

Local moves: you pay for time

Within roughly 100 miles, movers charge by the hour. 2026 guides put crew rates at about $100–$200 per hour (often with a 2–3 hour minimum). A typical 2–3 bedroom local move runs $1,200–$2,500 and takes 4–8 hours.

Long-distance moves: you pay for weight × distance

Interstate moves are priced on the weight of your shipment and the distance. A 2–3 bedroom home (5,000–7,500 lb) moving 1,000+ miles typically costs $4,500–$8,500. Per-pound benchmarks around $0.50–$0.70 appear for ~1,000-mile moves; longer routes and peak season push higher.

Six cost drivers

  1. Distance
  2. Weight — the biggest lever on interstate moves; declutter first.
  3. Packing services
  4. Valuation coverage
  5. Timing — summer (May–September) costs 15–30% more; mid-month, mid-week dates can save 25–40%.
  6. Specialty items — pianos, safes, pool tables add $150–$800.

How to save

  • Get 3–5 written estimates and compare valuation coverage, not just price.
  • Move off-peak; be flexible on dates.
  • Sell or donate heavy items you won't use.
  • Pack yourself but hire labor for loading.
