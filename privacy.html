Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

What we collect

  • Tool inputs (ZIPs, dates) are processed in your browser. ZIP queries are sent to Zippopotam.us to return results; we do not store them.
  • Form submissions (quote requests, contact, contests, applications, pledges, newsletter) — the details you type, the page URL and a timestamp. Forms are delivered through FormSubmit.co to our inbox.
  • Local storage on your device for preferences such as dark mode and checklist progress. You can clear it anytime.

How we use it

To answer you, deliver what you asked for, run contests, and — only when you request quotes and consent — share your move details with up to five matched partners. We never sell personal data to list brokers.

Advertising & cookies

When enabled, Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Embedded YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics collects aggregated usage data.

Your rights

Ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, via the contact form. Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), the EU/UK (GDPR) and Canada (PIPEDA) have additional rights, which we honor.

Retention & security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose (quote requests: 12 months). All traffic uses HTTPS.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and contests are 18+.
