Calculations

  • Distance: haversine, Earth radius 3,958.8 mi; road ≈ 1.2× straight line; drive time at 55 mph.
  • Prefix → city: the most frequent city among active ZIPs sharing a prefix.
  • Time zones: state-level assignment, with split-state notes.
  • Moving costs: see the estimator assumptions.

Known limitations

Open ZIP data includes most standard and PO-box ZIPs; some unique and military ZIPs may be missing, and ZIP boundaries change. US ZIP totals vary by source (Wikipedia cites 41,557 as of October 2025; zip-codes.com lists 41,517 in October 2026) because datasets differ on retired and special-purpose codes.
